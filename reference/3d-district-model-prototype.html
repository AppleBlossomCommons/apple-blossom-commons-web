      What this is

      District outlines traced from the masterplan's Districts sheet (jeffrey mak: architect ltd, July 2026) and raised over a greyscale copy of that sheet. Not a survey, a final design, or building massing.

      What it tests

      Whether a 3D model reads more clearly than a flat plan for the City and land buyers, whether it runs smoothly on a phone, and whether traced accuracy is good enough.

      What would improve it

      The original PDF page or the architect's vector or 3D files (sharper ground and exact edges), real terrain from public elevation data, and drone imagery.
