Chelan, Washington · Masterplan, July 2026

A community planned to grow with Chelan, over decades.

Apple Blossom Commons is a long-range plan for about 160 acres around the Lake Chelan Community Hospital campus: ten districts for every stage of life, a wine and culinary village, parks and open space, designed to be walkable and built in phases.

CONNECTED COMMUNITY

Planned around an established hospital campus.

The Wellness District brings together the Lake Chelan Community Hospital campus and Columbia Valley Community Health, with senior living in Orchard Grove and Heritage Grove a short walk away. Technology concepts under exploration aim to support residents' independence and the people who care for them, never to replace care.

Read the Connected Community vision →
A new cardiology service, three specialty physicians, expanded Express Care and a hospitalist program at Lake Chelan Health. Lake Chelan Health board letter · Jan 2026 · High
A new EMS and administration building on the campus, funded with $12M over two legislative sessions. Public record · 2026 · High
Clinics, therapy and medical assisted living planned within walking distance of the senior homes. Masterplan · Jul 2026 · Planning concept
WHO IS MOVING TO CHELAN

Chelan County gained adults aged 65 to 69 while King County lost them.

Net migration rates for the same age band over 2010 to 2020 run in opposite directions. No public dataset links a mover's age to the county they left, so this shows a pattern, not a proven flow.
