# Home (`/`)

Scope: the public Home page. Mode: Persuade. First shown to Andy (Chelan Village) on Oct 16, 2026 as a draft; City of Chelan credibility is the bar.

Audience and job: all four audiences arrive here; the page states the vision in one screen and routes each reader through a doorway (Planning with Chelan, Investment perspective, Development opportunities). Primary action: Explore the plan. Secondary: Become a Partner.

Proof and content: real district outlines and facts from `data/`; existing anchors (Lake Chelan Community Hospital campus, existing ball fields) as the "today" truth. No photography exists. The acreage figure carries a "Source TBC" chip until the owner names its source.

Constraints: Orchard Atlas (docs/04) is the pinned world. Motion explains space only: no parallax, loops, count-ups. The Today-to-Planned reveal is one of the two approved scroll/story moments. Disclaimer on the plan. HTML equivalent of the map. Full dark mode (owner, Oct 16, 2026).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The first screen is a before-and-after survey plate: the land as it stands today, and the ten planned districts revealed over it by a divider the visitor controls. It refuses the category's hero-photo-plus-tagline and the rejected cinematic 3D opener.

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper ground, ink hairlines, the plan in land-use family fills at reduced opacity, numbered Plex Mono roundels, Source Serif headlines, one Harvest Amber signal reserved for the divider and its handle, basalt footer.

STORY: The visitor sees what exists, watches what is planned appear around it, understands ten districts on one platform evolving over decades, then picks the doorway for their role.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left four columns: mono eyebrow, serif headline (~64px desktop), two-line sub, Explore the plan button plus Become a Partner link. Right eight columns: the plan stage, Today on the left of an amber divider, Planned on the right, disclaimer beneath. Doorways begin at the fold.

FORM: Structure 1 of 7 on the ranked list (Today, then Planned), dealt in the surface roll. seed:782970c35231a53c. Signature interaction: on load the planning-area outline draws, the existing anchors settle, then the divider sweeps once from Today to its resting point, uncovering districts whose roundels land as they are revealed; afterwards the visitor drags it (pointer, touch, arrow keys, Today/Planned buttons). Hovering or focusing a district, on the map or in the legend below, lifts it and names it. Reduced motion: no sweep, divider rests at center.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
