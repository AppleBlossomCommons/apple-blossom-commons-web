How to use

  1. Pause where a district is clearly in view.
  2. Pick a label (or press 1–9, 0), then click where it sits in the frame.
  3. Drag a pin to move it. Select it and press Delete to remove it.
  4. Move ahead a few seconds and press Make keyframe here. Your labels carry over; drag them onto the right places.
  5. Leave a label out of a keyframe to fade it out.
  6. Faint pins between keyframes are a rough preview. The render follows the ground with motion tracking.

Keys: Space play/pause · ← → frame · Shift ← → second · [ ] keyframe · K make keyframe
