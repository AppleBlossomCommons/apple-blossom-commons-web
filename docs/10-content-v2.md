## The Plan (/plan)

### Intro
- **Heading:** The plan
- **Text:** Apple Blossom Commons is a long-range plan for about 160 acres near Lake Chelan. It is meant to grow slowly, one neighborhood at a time, over many years. Each part is planned to work with the others: homes near care, a small village within walking distance, and parks and open space throughout.

### 3D model [existing model and list]
- Label: Illustrative 3D interpretation of the July 2026 masterplan. Not a survey or final design.

### How it fits together [short items]
- **Homes for every stage of life.** Cottages and townhomes for active adults, independent and assisted living, memory care, and family homes and apartments.
- **A small village.** Cafés, restaurants, tasting rooms and shops along a lane designed for walking, sized to add to downtown Chelan, not compete with it.
- **Parks and open space.** A recreation park, trails and a large orchard preserve.
- **Built in phases.** Each neighborhood comes when there is real demand for it. [Jump link "How the plan grows →" to #guiding-ideas, per `11-plan-and-districts-content.md`.]

### Care, close by [id="care-close-by"; was Connected Community]
- **Heading:** Care, close by
- **Text:** The senior neighborhoods, Orchard Grove and Heritage Grove, are planned within a short walk of the hospital campus. The Wellness District nearby is planned for clinics, therapy and medical-assisted living. [Name Lake Chelan Health only as a neighbor, per CLAUDE.md.]
- **Subheading:** Technology that supports people
- **Text:** We're exploring ideas like virtual visits with specialists, home sensors that can alert a caregiver, and walking paths designed for everyday health. These are ideas for supporting independence and caregivers. They will never replace the people who give care, and residents will decide how their own information is used.
- **Link:** Read the full vision → [/research, Connected Community summary]

### Why Chelan [id="why-chelan"; was Chelan & the Region]
- **Heading:** Why Chelan
- **Text:** Four seasons, the lake, orchards and vineyards, and a small-town pace. People come to Chelan for the same reasons they stay. The plan is shaped to protect what makes this place special: the views, the open space and a town that knows its neighbors.

### FAQs [id="faqs"; accordion]

**About the plan**
- **Who is behind Apple Blossom Commons?** Chelan Village, LP, which owns the land and is responsible for the long-term plan. We work with builders, operators and other partners to bring each neighborhood to life over many years.
- **Is the City a partner?** Chelan Village, LP is a private, long-term steward of this land. Like any proposal, the plan goes through Chelan's public review process.
- **Is the plan approved?** Parts of the land already have zoning in place, and each neighborhood still goes through review before it is built. [Andy to confirm wording.]
- **How fast will it be built?** Slowly. The plan is designed to grow in phases over many years, one neighborhood at a time, as demand is real.
- **When might the first homes or businesses open?** It's too early to say. We'll share timing as plans firm up.

**For neighbors**
- **Will this bring traffic and crowds?** The plan is built around neighborhoods designed for walking and phased growth, so change comes gradually. Traffic and road access are studied as part of the review process.
- **Who pays for roads, water and sewer upgrades?** It's too early to say. These questions are worked out during the review process, and we'll share answers as they're settled.
- **Will homes be used as short-term rentals?** It's too early to say. We know this matters to Chelan, and we'll share our approach as plans firm up.
- **Will there be housing for local workers and families?** The plan includes family homes and apartments as well as senior living. Details are still being worked out.
- **Will the parks and trails be open to the public?** It's too early to say. We'll share plans as the parks are designed.
- **What happens to the orchards and the views?** Protecting open space and scenic views is a core part of the plan, including a large orchard preserve.
- **How will wildfire and evacuation be handled?** The plan follows Firewise design principles. Emergency access is reviewed as part of the approval process.
- **Will it compete with downtown Chelan?** The village is sized to serve people who live nearby and to add to what downtown offers, not to replace it.

**For future residents and families**
- **Is it only for retirees?** No. The plan includes homes for active adults and seniors, and family homes and apartments.
- **Is senior living 55+? Will there be entrance fees?** It's too early to say. Join the update list to hear as plans firm up.
- **Can I stay if my care needs change?** That's the goal. Independent homes, assisted living and memory care are planned in one connected community.
- **How close is the hospital?** The senior neighborhoods are planned within a short walk of the hospital campus.
- **What will homes cost?** It's too early to say. We'll share pricing when homes are ready to offer.
- **How do I join the update list?** Use the form on this page.

**For partners**
- **How do I invest or buy land?** Visit Partner with Us and send an inquiry.
- **Can I see the detailed research?** Approved partners can access detailed reports. Start with an inquiry on Partner with Us.
