# 01 — Project brief

*As of October 16, 2026. Source: the Apple Blossom Commons Claude Project (research workspace).*

## What Apple Blossom Commons is

Apple Blossom Commons is a long-term community-building initiative on roughly 160 acres of coordinated landholdings in Chelan, Washington, planned over a horizon of about 15 years and beyond. It is meant as a walkable (by design), mixed-use Pacific Northwest community that supports residents through multiple stages of life and strengthens, rather than competes with, historic Downtown Chelan.

- **Chelan Village, LP** is the coordinating steward and master planner. It sets the framework that builders, operators, healthcare providers, hospitality partners and investors work within. It is not portrayed as a lot seller or a conventional developer.
- **Apple Blossom Center** is the land platform: the planning area, entitlements and infrastructure.
- **Apple Blossom Commons** is the community identity built on that platform. It is not a separate project.

## The core public story

The central idea is **a community where a strong medical system, technology and retirement living come together**, set within a full mixed-use community: housing for all ages, a wine and culinary village, hospitality, parks and four-season recreation.

The Wellness District, anchored by the existing hospital campus, is presented as an integral part of the community. The healthcare and technology story (Connected Community) is public, under the guardrails in `03-content-and-tiers.md`.

## The demand thesis (for context; public only in summary form)

- Chelan County's own population is not enough on its own to sustain senior communities at scale.
- Demand centers on Western Washington relocators, especially home-equity-driven relocation from King County and the Seattle–Tacoma–Bellevue area.
- Public evidence (state and federal data) shows the pattern but cannot prove a named origin-to-destination flow. Copy must say "pattern", not "proof".

## Ten districts (masterplan legend order)

1. Chelan Wine Village
2. Wellness District
3. Orchard Grove (55+ / age in place)
4. Heritage Grove (assisted living through memory care)
5. Apple Blossom Residence (market-rate, not senior housing)
6. Harvest Crossing (workforce housing, live-work)
7. Summit Recreation Park
8. Discovery Commons (aspirational)
9. Orchard Preserve
10. Grand Orchard Hotel

The full facts are in `data/districts.json`. Hatched areas on the masterplan are land outside the ten districts and are not labeled as Apple Blossom Commons.
